Every guide follows the same structure, so once you know one, you know them all.

## Levels

| Level | Name | You can... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Orientation | Name the parts, tools, and vocabulary, and know the safety basics. |
| 1 | Beginner | Complete common jobs by following a walkthrough. |
| 2 | Intermediate | Do routine work on your own and spot mistakes. |
| 3 | Advanced | Take on multi-part jobs and diagnose problems from symptoms. |
| 4 | Expert | Do professional-grade work. |
| 5 | Mastery | Handle rare and complex work, and explain why things work the way they do. |

In the tech guides, Level 5 goes down to internals and research papers. In the hands-on guides, it means master-level craft, and no page replaces the hours of practice that level takes.

## Walkthroughs

Walkthroughs are step-by-step jobs, graded **easy**, **moderate**, **hard**, or **expert**. Each lists the time, tools, and parts it needs, how to check the job worked, and its sources.

## Cheat sheets

Each guide has one cheat sheet in three tiers:

- **Common:** what you'll use on most jobs.
- **Uncommon:** specific situations you'll meet eventually.
- **Rare:** recovery, niche, or risky operations. Every rare entry links to its source.

## Page status

Every page carries a status. Anything short of verified shows a warning banner at the top.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Planned | A placeholder. Nothing is written yet. |
| Draft | Written but not fact-checked. Don't rely on it. |
| Review | Sources are being checked against the text. |
| Verified | Checked against the listed sources on the date shown. |

The site won't build if a page is marked verified without a verification date and at least one source.

## Safety

Pages marked safety-critical cover work where a mistake can hurt someone: brakes, mains electricity, TV power supplies, and medical skills. Specs such as torque values and fluid types always come from the manufacturer's service manual for your exact model, and these pages link to their sources rather than reproducing them.
